# Advanced Algorithms

- **Greedy Algorithms**
  - Greedy choice, intervals, scheduling, and resource allocation
- **Backtracking**
  - Decision trees, constraints, and pruning
  - Subsets, combinations, and permutations
- **Dynamic Programming**
  - Overlapping subproblems and optimal substructure
  - Memoization, tabulation, and space optimization
  - One-dimensional, two-dimensional, sequence, grid, and tree DP
- **Range-Query Data Structures**
  - Coordinate compression
  - Fenwick trees and segment trees
  - Range updates and lazy propagation
- **Advanced Graph Algorithms**
  - Strongly connected components
  - Bridges and articulation points
  - Advanced shortest-path techniques
- **Bit Manipulation**
  - Bitwise operations, shifts, masks, and XOR patterns
- **Mathematical Algorithms**
  - Prime numbers, GCD, LCM, and the Sieve of Eratosthenes
  - Modular arithmetic, fast exponentiation, and combinatorics
